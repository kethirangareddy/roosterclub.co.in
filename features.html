Everything in one app

Every feature, built for breeders

Rooster Club brings buying, selling, learning and running your farm into one trusted app, in your language. Here is everything it does.

Buy & sell

The marketplace

Sellers: birds, eggs and chicks near you

Browse listings by breed, type, price and distance. Each type has its own details (game, breeder, patta, layer and more), with photos, age, weight, colour and how far the seller delivers.

Buyers: post what you want

Can't find the bird you need? Post a request and sellers in your district are told about it, so the right seller comes to you.

A chat for every listing

WhatsApp-style chat with photos and voice notes. When a bird is sold or removed, everyone who asked sees it right away, with a "see similar" button.

Pre-book eggs and chicks

Reserve hatching eggs and chicks ahead of time. The seller sees every booking and you get a message when they are ready.

Rooster Club Shop

Feed in 10 kg sacks for chicks, growers, layers and breeders, laddus, ayurvedic powders and farm accessories, sold by Rooster Club with cash on delivery.

Trust

How we keep deals honest

KYC-verified sellers

Sellers verify with Aadhaar and a live blink selfie. Verified sellers get a badge, and their real name is shown.

Deal receipts

Only the buyer can ask for a receipt, and the seller confirms it. It records the bird, the price and the handover, so both sides have proof.

Medals and reviews

Honest sellers earn medals and reviews from real deals. Medals bring featured credits, never fake ratings.

Clean listings

Listings are for birds, eggs and chicks only. Fight videos and photos are removed, and you can report or block anyone.

Learn New

Rooster School

Picture lessons with voice

Short lessons with pictures and a Telugu voice, in chapters: Aseel, Indian breeds, gamebirds of the world, colours, chick care, feed, health, natural care, diseases, medicines and breeding.

Breeding, made clear

Line breeding, father × daughter, outcross, top cross, clan mating and three-line plans, each shown as a correct chart with the blood share of every generation.

Levels, quizzes and certificates

Beginner, Expert and Master levels. Pass each chapter's quiz to earn its badge, your wreath and a certificate. The Beginner level is free after KYC.

Breeder Pro

Bird Doctor

Answer simple questions about a sick or dead bird and see what it could be and what to do next.

Feed Maker and Make My Feed

Pick the feeds you have and their prices, and get the best mix with quantities. Or design your own formula and get it milled in 10 kg sacks.

Breeding Book and Breeding Planner

Record your birds, plan safe matings across generations and see what to expect from every pair.

Hatch Tracker and My flock

Hatch reminders on days 7, 14, 18 and 21. Chicks move into My flock and change age group on their own.

Today on my farm

Your jobs for today, the flock calendar, vaccine and deworming dates, and weather alerts.

Farm khata

Money in and out, feed stock and egg log, in one simple book with a monthly report.

Ask Rooster Club

Ask any question about your birds and get an answer in your language.
