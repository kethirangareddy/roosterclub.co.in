Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order any time before it is dispatched by messaging us on WhatsApp +91 95153 69756 or emailing ranga@roosterclub.co.in with your order number. If you paid online, you get a full refund. Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled.

Printed T-shirts are made to order. They can be cancelled only before printing starts (usually within 12 hours of the order).

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your product arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, tell us within 48 hours of delivery with photos (an unboxing video helps). After checking, we will send a replacement or give a full refund, including the delivery charge.

Returns

  • Accessories (incubators, brooder kits, feeders, rings, carry boxes): can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in the original packing. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item was damaged, defective or wrong.
  • Feed and feed supplements: cannot be returned once the seal is opened, for hygiene and safety reasons, unless damaged or wrong.
  • Printed T-shirts: made to order, so they cannot be returned or exchanged for size; only damaged or wrong prints are replaced. Please check the size chart before ordering.

Warranty

Products with a warranty (shown on the product page) are repaired or replaced if they stop working because of a manufacturing fault within the warranty period.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within 5–7 working days. For cash on delivery orders, refunds are sent by UPI or bank transfer to the account you give us.

Contact

WhatsApp +91 95153 69756 or email ranga@roosterclub.co.in.
