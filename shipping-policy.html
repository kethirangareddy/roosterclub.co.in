Delivery & Shipping Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver to most pin codes across India through our courier partners. If a pin code cannot be served, we will contact you before dispatch and cancel the order with a full refund if it was prepaid.

Dispatch time

Orders are packed and dispatched from Hyderabad within 1–2 working days of confirmation. The dispatch time shown on a product page applies to that product. Printed T-shirts are made to order and are dispatched within 3–5 working days.

Delivery time

Delivery usually takes 3–7 working days after dispatch. Remote areas can take up to 10 working days. Delays caused by the courier, weather, strikes or holidays are outside our control, but we will help you follow up.

Delivery charges

Delivery is free on orders of ₹999 or more. For smaller orders a delivery charge of ₹49 is added. Some products include delivery in their price; this is shown on the product page. The full amount is shown before you place the order.

Payment

You can pay online or choose cash on delivery (where available). For cash on delivery, please pay the delivery person the amount shown on your order.

Tracking

Once your order ships, the courier name and tracking number appear on your order page and are sent to you by SMS or WhatsApp.

Wrong address or not available

Please check your address and phone number before ordering. If a parcel comes back because the address was wrong or nobody was available after the courier's attempts, we will contact you to arrange delivery again; the extra delivery charge may apply.
