The marketplace

Built for trust, not WhatsApp-group chaos

No fake sellers, no lost records, no middlemen. Every seller can be verified, every chat stays with its listing, and every deal can end with a receipt.

KYC-verified sellers

Sellers verify with Aadhaar and a live selfie. You see who you are dealing with before you travel or pay.

Deal receipts

The buyer asks for a receipt and the seller confirms it, with the bird's details and a handover record, so both sides have proof.

Sellers and Buyers near you

Browse birds by breed, type and distance, or post what you want in Buyers and let sellers in your district come to you.

A chat for every listing

Talk to sellers like on WhatsApp, with voice notes and photos. When a bird is sold, everyone who asked is told.

Pre-book eggs and chicks

Reserve hatching eggs and chicks ahead of time. The seller sees your booking and you get a message when they're ready.

Medals and reviews

Honest sellers earn medals and reviews from real deals. Medal holders get featured, never through paid fake ratings.

Follow breeders

Follow farms you like and see their birds, posts and reels in your feed, with stories like Instagram.

Alerts for your district

Get told about new birds nearby, disease outbreaks and theft reports in your area, with quiet hours you choose.

Rooster School Coming soon

Learn breeding the way the best breeders do

Every lesson is short, shown in pictures and read aloud in Telugu. It starts from the Aseel and goes all the way to line breeding, clan mating and bird health.

  • Breeding, step by stepFather × daughter, line breeding, outcross, top cross and three-line plans, each drawn as a clear chart.
  • Health, feed and chick careDiseases, vaccines, medicines, natural care and feed you can mix from what you already grow.
  • Levels and certificatesBeginner, Expert and Master. Pass each chapter's quiz to earn its badge and your wreath.

The Beginner level (Aseel, Indian breeds, gamebirds of the world, colours) is free after KYC.

Breeder Pro Coming soon

Your whole farm, in your pocket

Tools built for Indian breeders: simple enough for the yard, strong enough for a serious breeding programme.

Bird Doctor

Answer simple questions about a sick or dead bird and see what it could be and what to do next.

Feed Maker

Pick the grains you have and their prices. Get the best mix with quantities, and what to add.

Breeding Book

Record your birds, plan matings safely and see the blood share of every chick.

Hatch Tracker

Reminders on days 7, 14, 18 and 21, then move the chicks into My flock in one tap.

Flock calendar

Vaccines, deworming and jobs for today on your farm, with weather alerts.

Farm khata

Money in and out, feed stock and egg log, all in one simple book.

Make My Feed

Design your own feed formula and get it milled in 10 kg sacks (Telangana and Andhra Pradesh).
