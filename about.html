About Us

Rooster Club · Hyderabad, Telangana

Rooster Club is a Hyderabad-based business that helps backyard and country-chicken (desi poultry) farmers across India raise healthier birds. We run this website, the Rooster Club app and an online shop.

What we sell

Through our online shop we sell our own products and ship them across India by courier:

  • Poultry accessories: egg incubators, brooder heat kits, chick feeders and drinkers, leg rings, bird care kits and carry boxes
  • Poultry feed and feed supplements (for animal use only)
  • Rooster Club branded T-shirts

Every product page shows the price in Indian Rupees, what is in the box and how to use it. Customers can pay online or choose cash on delivery.

Who we are

Rooster Club is a proprietorship owned by Kethireddy Ranga Reddy, registered as a micro enterprise under Udyam (UDYAM-TS-02-0363699). Our founder has a background in animal nutrition and feed.
